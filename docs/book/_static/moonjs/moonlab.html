Backlit dark side = the device path (dark_zero=1: the unilluminated surface computed from albedo + relief, lit from −S). Toggle it off to see the validated earthshine model (dark_zero=0, the image-gate path). Warm tint = the display's gray→RGB565 LUT (R×1.038 / B×0.933); off = neutral. K, the soft terminator, and relief all act on the displayed bake exactly as they do in moon_bake.c.
